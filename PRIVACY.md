# Privacy Policy for MESA (Machine Literacy Enhanced Study Assistant)

**Last Updated:** April 2026

Thank you for using MESA! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our browser extension.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
MESA is designed to respect your privacy. The extension only accesses the content of the active web page or PDF when you explicitly interact with it (e.g., clicking "Summarize" or asking a question). 

- **Page Content:** When you request a summary or ask a question, the text of the current page is sent securely to our backend proxy to be processed by the selected AI model (Kimi, Gemini, or OpenAI). We do not permanently store this text.
- **Local Storage:** The extension stores your preferences (like your preferred AI model) locally on your device using browser storage APIs. This data is not transmitted to us.

### 2. Third-Party Services
MESA utilizes third-party AI services to generate responses:
- **Kimi API (via NVIDIA)**
- **Google Gemini API**
- **OpenAI API**

When you use the extension, the necessary text is sent to these services. Please review their respective privacy policies to understand how they handle data.

### 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or browsing data to outside parties.

### 4. Changes to this Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
